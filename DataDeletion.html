Data Deletion Request

TaxiPro lets users delete the app data stored locally on their device. This page explains what can be deleted and how to request it.

What can be deleted

  • Trip history
  • Shift history
  • Route statistics
  • Zone data created in the app
  • Local settings and preferences

How to delete your data

  1. Open TaxiPro.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Choose Reset / Clear data.
  4. Confirm the deletion.
If you need help, contact: statslab.dev@gmail.com

What may be retained

Some data may remain only if it is required for legal, tax, accounting, or fraud-prevention purposes, or if it is handled by Google Play for a purchase or subscription.
