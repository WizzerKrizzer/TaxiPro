TaxiPro Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

TaxiPro ("the app", "we", "our", or "us") helps drivers track rides, shifts, routes, earnings, statistics, and backups. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app may access, how it is used, and how it is handled.

1. Information the app accesses or processes

Depending on the features you use, TaxiPro may access or process the following data:

  • Location data: precise and approximate location, including background location while ride or shift tracking is active.
  • Route and address data: locations, route points, and addresses used for ride tracking, route calculation, and zone features.
  • Ride and shift data: fares, tips, distance, duration, waiting time, notes, zones, tariffs, expenses, and statistics entered or generated inside the app.
  • Purchase status: information needed to determine premium access and subscriptions or lifetime purchases through Google Play Billing.
  • Device/network state: basic network availability used for map, directions, and geocoding related features.
  • Notification permission status: used so the app can show tracking-related notifications when enabled.

2. How the app uses information

TaxiPro uses data only to provide app features such as:

  • tracking rides and shifts;
  • calculating distance, time, waiting, and earnings;
  • displaying maps, routes, and zones;
  • generating statistics, reports, and summaries;
  • creating backups and export files when requested by the user;
  • managing premium purchases and subscription status.

3. Data storage

TaxiPro primarily stores user-entered and generated app data locally on the user's device (for example in the app database and app settings storage). Backup and PDF export files are created only when the user requests them.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell user data.

The app may share limited data with service providers only when required for app functionality, such as:

  • Google Play Billing for processing premium purchases and subscriptions.
  • Google Maps / geolocation / directions related services for map display, route calculation, and address/location lookup.

Backup files, PDF reports, or other exported files are shared only if the user explicitly chooses to export or share them.

5. Background location

TaxiPro may access background location only to support active ride and shift tracking features when the user enables and uses them. Background location is used to continue measuring travel distance and related ride or shift statistics while tracking is active.

6. Data retention and deletion

Data stored locally remains on the device until the user deletes it, clears the app's data, uninstalls the app, or restores another backup over the existing data.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect data processed by the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Children's privacy

TaxiPro is not designed for children under 13.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
